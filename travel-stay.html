Travel

🚗 By car

There is on-site parking available at Hoath House, with space for up to approximately 70 cars. Upon arrival, please follow the directional arrows and wedding signage, which will guide you to the designated car park. We recommend allowing a little extra time for your journey, especially if travelling during busy periods.

🚆 By train

The nearest railway station is Cowden Station, which is approximately a short taxi journey from Hoath House. Hever Station is also another nearby option. As the venue is located in the countryside, we recommend arranging your onward journey from the station in advance.

🚌 By bus

Local bus services operate in the surrounding area, although services are limited and there is no direct bus to Hoath House itself. We recommend checking timetables ahead of your journey and arranging alternative transport if needed.

🚕 Taxis

As our venue is in the Kent countryside, we strongly recommend pre-booking your taxi for the end of the evening — taxis may be limited if booked on the night, particularly during busy summer weekends.

✈️ Travelling from further away

For guests travelling from further afield, there are several airport options — London Gatwick Airport is the closest major airport and our recommended option.

Please be sure to plan your onward journey to Hoath House in advance, as the venue is situated in the beautiful Kent countryside.
