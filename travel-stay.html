Getting there

Travel

🚗 By car

The Grove Ferry Inn is located just outside Canterbury in Kent. Parking is outside and free — if the car park is full, there is a pay-as-you-go option next to it.

🚆 By train

Sturry Station — the closest, approximately 4 miles away; continue by taxi or local bus.

Canterbury West Station — approximately 8 miles away, with excellent connections including direct high-speed services from London St Pancras International. Continue to the venue by taxi or bus via the city centre.

🚌 By bus

There are local bus services connecting Canterbury with the Grove Ferry/Upstreet area. As services and timetables may change before the wedding, we recommend checking your journey closer to the date.

🚕 Taxis

As our venue is just outside Canterbury, we strongly recommend pre-booking your taxi for the end of the evening — taxis may be limited if booked on the night, particularly during busy summer weekends.

✈️ Travelling from further away

We know some of you will be travelling a little further to celebrate with us, and we truly appreciate you making the journey! Canterbury is a great base for the weekend, with plenty of accommodation, restaurants and transport links nearby.

From London: Canterbury West is easily accessible from London St Pancras International via direct high-speed services.

By air: London Gatwick and London Heathrow are the main airport options, with onward connections to Canterbury by train, coach or car.
