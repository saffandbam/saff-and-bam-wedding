There’s a second CSS selector sitting inside the curly braces of the first one. CSS has no concept of “nested rules” like that (that’s a Sass/SCSS feature, not plain CSS) — so a browser parsing this just sees garbage after margin:0; and ignores everything from that point to the final closing brace.
