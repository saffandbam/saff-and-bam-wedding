❓ Good to know

Questions

Can I bring a plus one?

Absolutely! Plus ones are more than welcome to join us for the reception celebrations. If you would also like your plus one to attend the ceremony, please let us know — we'll be happy to see what we can do and work around numbers where possible. We just ask that you let us know as soon as possible, so we can make sure everyone is accounted for.

Are children invited?

Absolutely! Our littlest guests are more than welcome to celebrate with us. We want the day to be just as enjoyable for them as it is for the adults, so there will be plenty of activities and entertainment to keep them occupied throughout the day while the grown-ups celebrate too!

What should I wear?

We'd love for our guests to dress up and celebrate with us in formal wedding attire — think summer dresses, suits, jumpsuits and your favourite excuse to get a little dressed up! We kindly ask that guests avoid white, ivory and cream, as well as the colours reserved for our bridal party (see the Dress Code for the palette). Other than that, wear something you feel amazing in!

Is there parking?

Yes and yes! There is on-site parking available at the venue, and you're more than welcome to leave your car overnight if you'd rather enjoy the celebrations and arrange a taxi home. Please just remember to come back and collect it the following day!

What time should I arrive?

We'll confirm exact arrival timings closer to the day — for now, tentative guest arrival is 11:00am with the ceremony starting at 12:00pm.

What time does the wedding finish?

We'll be celebrating well into the night! Last orders will be at 1:00am, so there's plenty of time to dance, celebrate and make the most of the evening with us. Please make sure any taxis or lifts home are pre-booked in advance so you can enjoy the night without worrying about getting home!

Can I take photos during the ceremony?

Absolutely! We'd love for you to capture little moments from the day and make memories with us. Feel free to take as many photos as you like throughout the celebrations, and don't forget to share your favourites with us afterwards!

Can I post pictures on social media?

Absolutely! We'd love for you to share your favourite moments from our special day. Feel free to post, tag us and share as many memories as you'd like — we can't wait to see the day through everyone else's eyes too!

Is the venue accessible?

Yes! The venue is accessible, and we want to make sure all of our guests are able to enjoy the day as comfortably as possible. If you have any specific accessibility requirements or need a little extra assistance, please let us know in advance and we'll be more than happy to help make arrangements for you.

Is there somewhere to stay nearby?

Absolutely — see our Travel & Stay page for our recommendations. If you're struggling to find somewhere suitable or need a little help choosing the best location, please don't hesitate to get in touch, we'll be more than happy to help!
